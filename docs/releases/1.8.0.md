## CMO Scenario Studio 1.8.0 — The Course Window

### New: plan a route leg by leg

Courses now open in their own window. Open it with **Edit course...** in the unit inspector, **Course...** on the
map toolbar, or by right-clicking a unit on the map.

- **A map of the route**: add waypoints by clicking, insert one half-way along a leg, drag, reorder, reverse or
  remove them. The selected waypoint is ringed; click a waypoint to select its leg.
- **A table of legs**: altitude, speed, radar and weapons in force, waits, leg length and estimated arrival time
  (H+h:mm) for every leg, plus the way home or to the landing base.
- **A flight profile**: altitude and speed along the whole route - climb-out, low run-in, descent to land - with
  strips for radar, jammers (sonar for ships and submarines) and weapons on each leg. Click it to select a leg.
- **Settings for each leg**:
  - name, altitude (with feet), speed;
  - radar, sonar and jammers on or off;
  - weapons free, tight or hold;
  - whether the unit may leave the course to attack;
  - **Wait there**: circle the waypoint for a set time (a tanker, a holding point, a time on target);
  - **Signal there**: set a flag on arrival, so an event with **Flag is set** can react.

  **Same for every leg** copies one leg's settings to all. A setting applies on the leg flown *to* its waypoint and
  stays in force until a later leg changes it.
- **OK** keeps the course as one change (Ctrl+Z undoes it); **Cancel** asks before throwing changes away.

### In CMO

The *Studio: unit courses* event now applies each leg's settings as the leg starts (altitude, speed, EMCON, weapon
control, attack behaviour), circles at waypoints that have a wait, and sets signals on arrival. Progress is still
saved with the game. A setting CMO refuses is written to the Lua log and the rest of the course carries on.

### Changed

- The inspector's **Course** box is now a one-line summary with **Edit course...** and **Clear**.
- The map toolbar's **Plot course** is now **Course...**, which opens the window.
- The Timeline shows an estimated *en route* bar after each unit's departure.
- The Issues panel checks signal names and altitudes given to ships.
- The User Manual's courses section (Chapter 14) is rewritten around the window, with a worked strike example.

Projects from 1.7.0 open unchanged; their courses simply have no leg settings yet.
