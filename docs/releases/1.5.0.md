## CMO Scenario Studio 1.5.0 — Unit Templates

### New: Unit templates

Pre-grouped sites you name, orient and drop on the map in one go: **80 templates, 670 units in all**, built from
generic DB3000 facilities.

- **Airfields (single unit):** 12 sizes from heliport and 900 m airstrip to 3x 4,000 m hub, with tower, radar, fuel,
  munitions and barracks.
- **Airfields (multi unit):** tactical fighter base (runway, taxiway, 12 hardened shelters, ops bunker, fuel farm,
  munitions, AAA and SAM), main operating base (2x 3,200 m), strategic bomber base, hardened mountain base, helicopter
  base, road base, expeditionary strip, FARP and civil airport.
- **Command and control:** battalion and brigade command posts, strategic C3 bunker, air defence sector operations
  centre, mobile air defence control post, ICBM launch control.
- **Communications and EW:** military communications centre and bunker, radio/TV station, cellular towers, SIGINT
  and hardened intelligence complexes, SOSUS shore station, jamming site, mobile ELINT, navigation aids.
- **Radar and air defence:** early-warning radar, coastal surveillance, SAM site, AD battalion, AAA, aerostat.
- **Garrisons and bases:** company barracks, battalion garrison, FOB, combat outpost, observation post, desert fort,
  UN checkpoint.
- **Logistics:** ammunition, fuel and aviation fuel depots, supply hub, rail yard, field hospital, defended bridge,
  pontoon crossing.
- **Naval:** small and major naval bases, submarine base, commercial port, anchorage, coastal missile battery.
- **Missiles and drones, ground forces and infrastructure:** missile and drone sites, battlegroup, infantry and
  artillery positions, power stations, dam, refinery, oil field, government district, town, village, training range.

**Placing:** Tools > Place unit template (Ctrl+T), the map's *Templates...* button, or right-click the map. Choose a
site name, side, position and orientation with a to-scale preview. Units are named after the site
(*Amari Control Tower*, *Amari HAS 1*...) and grouped: dragging any part moves the whole site, the Formations tab
rotates it, and it builds as one CMO group. Parts that host aircraft or boats are marked; generic parts can be
swapped for national systems with *Use for selected unit*.

**Your own templates:** Tools > Template library edits, imports, exports and resets your own `templates.json`, and
*Save as unit template* (Units tab, right-click) turns any selection into a template. The format and full catalogue
are in `docs/TEMPLATES.md`.

### Also

- Formations can be grouped in CMO without formation stations (used for fixed sites).

### Download

**`CMO-Scenario-Studio-1.5.0-win64.zip`**: unzip anywhere and run `CMO-Scenario-Studio.exe`; no Python needed.
Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and a Command: Modern Operations installation. Projects from earlier versions open
unchanged.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe is not code-signed: choose *More info > Run anyway*.
