## CMO Scenario Studio 1.11.0 — Take-off Fix and Intercept Missions

### Fixed: aircraft at template air bases never took off

Aircraft were based and assigned but waited for a taxiway for ever. CMO moves an aircraft from its parking to a
*runway access point* (or taxiway) and then to the runway; all 46 UK air bases, the road base and the expeditionary
strip had no access points.

- **Templates** now have runway access points, sized for the largest aircraft each base can park.
- **Scenarios you have already made**: open the project and accept the offer to add them, or use **Tools > Repair air
  bases...** (one undo). Then **export and rebuild** the scenario in CMO.
- **The Issues panel and the dry-run** name every aircraft that could never take off and why: no access point, one
  too small, no runway or too short a one, or a base that is not one CMO group.

**RAF Trimingham** was not built because CMO's terrain puts its old position underwater; new placements use the
position in CMO's own UK radar file. Delete and place it again in existing projects.

### New: intercept missions

A new mission class, **Intercept**: aircraft stay on call at a base, like a real quick reaction alert, and launch only
when the conditions you tick are met.

- **About the contact** (all or any of them): inside an area, within a distance of the base or a chosen unit, hostile /
  unfriendly / unknown / neutral, heading towards you, altitude, speed, not identified, emitting, tracked for a time;
  optionally missiles too.
- **Also required, if ticked**: a raid size, a time window, or a flag.
- **Response**: aircraft per contact, contacts at once, aircraft kept in reserve, orders (identify, engage if hostile,
  engage), radars, when to stand down, messages, and a flag to set on launch.
- Presets for quick reaction alert, base defence and a bomber raid, and a plain-English summary.
- Shown on the Timeline and in the briefing pack, checked by the Issues panel, and covered by the manual (Chapter 14).

A *Strike / Air* mission with no targets is launched by CMO at the first air contact of any kind; the Issues panel now
says so. Change such missions to **Intercept** to choose when they launch.

### Known limits

The intercept manager is tested against the studio's stand-in for CMO's scripting; it has not been run in CMO itself yet. If CMO
will not take a contact as a mission target, the Lua log says so and nothing is launched. Please report anything odd on
the project's GitHub issues page.
