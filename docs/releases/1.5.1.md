## CMO Scenario Studio 1.5.1 — Patrol Area Library

### New: Predefined patrol areas

**134 ready-made sea and air patrol areas** (94 sea, 40 air), grouped by region of the globe:

| Region | Sea | Air |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Europe | 30 | 14 |
| Arctic | 7 | 3 |
| Middle East | 9 | 5 |
| Africa | 5 | 3 |
| Asia | 19 | 7 |
| Oceania | 7 | 2 |
| North America | 9 | 4 |
| South America | 8 | 2 |

- **Sea:** Bay of Biscay, Western Approaches, the Channel, North Sea, Baltic gulfs, Norwegian Sea, GIUK and
  Faroes-Shetland gaps, Gibraltar and the Mediterranean basins, Black Sea, Barents and Kola approaches, Bear Gap,
  Persian Gulf, Hormuz, Gulf of Oman, Red Sea and Bab-el-Mandeb, Gulf of Aden, Gulf of Guinea, Taiwan and Luzon
  straits, South and East China Seas, Sea of Japan and Okhotsk, Malacca and the Indonesian straits, Bay of Bengal,
  Coral and Tasman Seas, Gulf of Mexico, Caribbean, US fleet operating areas, Falklands TEZ, Drake Passage and more.
- **Air:** Baltic and Iceland air policing, eastern-flank CAPs, Kola and NORAD intercept zones, Gulf, Levant and Red
  Sea CAPs, Korean DMZ, Taiwan and East China Sea ADIZ, Okinawa, Hokkaido, Kashmir and Ladakh, AEW orbits, tanker
  tracks and the Nevada ranges.

**Adding:** Tools > Add patrol area from library (**Ctrl+Shift+A**), the map's *Patrol areas...* button or
right-click, *From library...* on the Areas tab, or type the name into Ctrl+K search. Filter by region, sea/air or
words, check the map preview, pick the side and name, and optionally create a mission on it - suggested per area
(ASW or ASuW patrol for sea, CAP for air, support for AEW orbits and tanker tracks). Ctrl/Shift-click adds several
at once, as one undo step.

**Your own areas:** Tools > Area library edits, imports, exports and resets your own `areas.json` (drag corners on
the map), and *Save to area library...* on any area you draw keeps it for other scenarios. Format and full list in
`docs/AREAS.md`.

### Download

**`CMO-Scenario-Studio-1.5.1-win64.zip`**: unzip anywhere and run `CMO-Scenario-Studio.exe`; no Python needed.
Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and a Command: Modern Operations installation. Projects from earlier versions open
unchanged.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe is not code-signed: choose *More info > Run anyway*.
