## CMO Scenario Studio 1.7.0 — Plotted Courses

### New: plot a course for any unit

Draw the exact route a unit takes, waypoint by waypoint, to script a computer side's movements without a mission:
a bomber stream on a set track, a convoy along a coast, or a pair of fighters that **take off from one base, fly a
route and land at another**.

- **On the map**: select a unit, click **Plot course** on the toolbar (or right-click the unit > **Plot
  course...**) and click the waypoints. Finish with a double-click or Enter. The course is drawn with numbered
  waypoints you can drag.
- **In the unit inspector**, a new **Course** box:
  - lists the waypoints, with an optional altitude and speed for each leg;
  - removes, reorders, reverses or clears them;
  - sets **Depart** (minutes after the start);
  - sets **At the end**: carry on, return to its base, **land at another base**, or fly the course again (loop).
- **Aircraft in a base** wait on the ground until their departure time, take off, fly the course, then return or
  land at the base you chose. Every aircraft of a squadron flies it. Ships, ground units and aircraft already in
  the air get their course at the build; reinforcements get theirs when they arrive.
- **In CMO**, a new event, *Studio: unit courses*, runs every minute of game time and keeps each unit's progress
  in key values, so saved games carry on correctly. The dry-run (F5) plays it through.
- **The Timeline** shows when each unit departs (amber rows).
- **The Issues panel** checks courses:
  - a facility with a course (it cannot move);
  - waypoints off the map;
  - a departure after the scenario ends;
  - a landing base of another side, or without room for the aircraft;
  - a unit that also has a mission or follows a formation lead.

### Changed

- The map toolbar's **Labels**, **Areas**, **Ref points**, **Grid** tick boxes and **Sides shown** are now one
  **Show ▾** menu, which also has the new **Courses** layer. This keeps the toolbar on one line.
- The User Manual explains courses in Chapter 14, with a worked Lossiemouth-to-Coningsby example. The Reference
  Manual documents `STUDIO.ROUTES`, `StudioRoutesTick` and `StudioUnderWay`.

Projects from earlier versions open unchanged; units simply have no course.
