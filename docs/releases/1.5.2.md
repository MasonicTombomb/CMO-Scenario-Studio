## CMO Scenario Studio 1.5.2 — UK Military Sites and Airspace

### New: UK military sites at their real locations

**101 templates of real UK sites** (1575 units) in the *UK - ...* template categories.

- **46 air bases** - RAF, Fleet Air Arm, Army Air Corps and USAF bases in the UK, plus relief landing grounds -
  laid out from OpenStreetMap: 82 runways at their real position, heading and length, 108 hardened aircraft
  shelters, 286 hangars sized by footprint, aprons as parking, control towers, ATC radars, fuel farms and
  munitions bunkers. Lossiemouth, Coningsby, Marham, Lakenheath, Mildenhall, Brize Norton, Culdrose, Yeovilton,
  Odiham, Wattisham and more.
- **Naval bases** (Faslane, Coulport, Devonport, Portsmouth, Rosyth, Barrow, Marchwood), **radar and air defence**
  (Fylingdales, Boulmer CRC, the remote radar heads, Sky Sabre at Thorney Island), **command and control** (Air
  Command, Northwood, MOD Main Building), **communications and intelligence** (Menwith Hill, GCHQ, Croughton, Digby,
  Anthorn VLF), **nuclear** (AWE Aldermaston and Burghfield), **munitions depots** and **garrisons**.
- In the placer (Ctrl+T), **At the real site** puts the whole base where it really is; untick it to reuse the layout
  elsewhere. Crossing runways keep their own headings.

Where OpenStreetMap does not map a base's control tower or hangars, the template adds one representative part and
says so in its description; some bases' shelters are not mapped (e.g. Coningsby), so their templates show only
mapped hangars. Air base layouts are © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) - see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

### New: UK airspace areas with official boundaries

A **United Kingdom** region in the area library (74 areas):

- **Official UK AIP boundaries** (AIRAC 2026-10-29, tagged *official*): the London FIR (QRA South area), the Scottish
  FIR (QRA North area) and the whole UK FIR; the danger areas of the Spadeadam, Tain, Cape Wrath, Hebrides, Holbeach,
  Donna Nook, Aberporth, Salisbury Plain and Otterburn ranges; and the air-to-air refuelling areas AARA 1-14.
- **Notional areas** (no published definition): eight interception sectors cut from the official FIRs that together
  cover the whole of UK airspace exactly once, approach zones (Iceland-Faroes gap, Norwegian Sea, Atlantic, southern
  North Sea), 18 CAP stations, AEW orbits, low-flying areas and UK naval exercise areas.

### Also

- Templates support a real-world location, per-part headings and real runway lengths; bundled template packs
  (`templates_*.json`) are merged into the library.
- New built-in templates and areas are merged into your own library files after an update; ones you deleted stay
  deleted.

### Download

**`CMO-Scenario-Studio-1.5.2-win64.zip`**: unzip anywhere and run `CMO-Scenario-Studio.exe`; no Python needed.
Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and a Command: Modern Operations installation. Projects from earlier versions open
unchanged.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the exe is not code-signed: choose *More info > Run anyway*.
