## CMO Scenario Studio 1.6.2 — Aircraft Base Fix

### Fixed: aircraft missing after the build

Aircraft based in something that cannot hold aircraft - a **runway**, taxiway or **control tower** (easy to pick
on a template air base), a facility too small for them, or a shelter that is already full - were refused by CMO
during the build. The build log showed a .NET `LuaScriptException` for each one and wrongly said
*"loadout ... refused, placed in reserve instead"*, and the aircraft were missing from the scenario.

In CMO, aircraft can only start in a **hangar**, **hardened aircraft shelter**, **apron** or **airfield** (or a
ship's hangar or deck) that fits them and has room. Now:

- **The build recovers**: it tries the other hangars, shelters and aprons of the same base and reports
  *"MONARCH 11: Lossiemouth Runway 05/23 cannot take it, based at Lossiemouth Hangar (4x Large) 1 instead"*. A big
  squadron on a one-aircraft shelter spreads over the base's shelters and hangars.
- **Honest reporting**: *loadout refused* only appears when the reserve loadout is what made it work; otherwise
  the report names the base as the cause.
- **The Issues panel** shows an error for aircraft whose base cannot hold them and a warning when more aircraft
  start at a base than it has room for.
- **Add to selected base** will not put aircraft on a runway or tower; on a template base it offers that base's
  hangars, shelters and aprons with room, and suggests a squadron size that fits.
- **The dry-run (F5)** checks aircraft space as CMO does, so the problem shows up before you build in CMO.

**Existing projects**: open them and look at the Issues panel; set **Starts at** of any flagged aircraft to a
hangar or shelter with room, or simply rebuild - the build now moves them for you.
