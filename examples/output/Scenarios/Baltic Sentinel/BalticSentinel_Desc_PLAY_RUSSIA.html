Scenario By: CMO Scenario Studio example

Date: May 12th 2030
Location: Gulf of Finland and the Estonian coast
Playable Side: Russia (NATO is computer-controlled)
Duration: 12 Hours

This is a hypothetical scenario made to demonstrate CMO Scenario Studio.

Tension in the Gulf of Finland has been rising for weeks. A Russian surface action group has sailed from Kronstadt, and Russian aircraft are probing Finnish and Estonian airspace.

NATO commands a Finnish and Estonian force under Allied air cover. Russia must show the flag, test the Alliance's air defences and, if ordered, seize the island of Hogland (Suursaari).

In this version NATO is commanded by the computer.
