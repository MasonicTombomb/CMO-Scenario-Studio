Situation

A Russian surface action group of two Steregushchiy corvettes and a Karakurt missile ship is in the eastern Gulf of Finland, covered by Su-35S fighters and an S-400 battalion near Kingisepp.

Mission

Keep Allied airspace and the sea lanes open, and keep Russian warships away from Hogland.

Execution

  • Weapons tight in the air until the enemy shows hostile intent.
  • Finnish F-35As can be readied after three hours (75% chance).

Command and Signal

+10 points per Russian unit destroyed, +1 every 30 minutes Hogland stays free.
